The dashboard gives every active account its own card, currency, current balance, and month-to-date money flow. Below the accounts, expense and income charts show how your finances change over a selected period.

<ScreenshotPair desktop={desktop} mobile={mobile} alt="Renalo dashboard with account balance cards" />

## What the numbers mean

**Total balance** starts with the account's opening balance, adds posted income and incoming transfers, subtracts posted expenses and outgoing transfers, and includes manual balance adjustments. Renalo uses today's date in your browser's timezone, so activity after your local today is excluded.

**Inflow** includes this month's posted income and incoming transfer amount for that account. **Outflow** includes posted expenses and outgoing transfers. The current month also follows your browser's timezone. Transfers use the amount belonging to each side's account currency.

Archived accounts are absent from the dashboard but keep their records.

## Explore income and expenses

The dashboard initially shows **Last 12 months**. Use the date selector below the account cards to choose a month, the last 12 months, the current year, all time, or a custom range. The dashboard remembers this selection in your browser. Relative selections such as **This month**, **Last 12 months**, and **This year** are saved as relative choices, so they move forward automatically when you return later; custom dates remain fixed.

Dashboard ranges stop at today in your browser's timezone. Future dates cannot be selected here, and planned transactions are excluded even for **This year** and **All time**. Expense, income, and transfer overviews still allow future ranges for planning.

The expense and income charts use the same default-currency projections as their overview pages and automatically group totals by day, week, or month. The dashed line is a linear trend that makes the overall direction easier to see. Transactions without an available default-currency conversion are omitted from these charts. Use the maximize button in a chart's top-right corner to open it across the full page.

## Record something quickly

Use **Record new** to open an expense, income, or transfer form without first visiting its overview.
