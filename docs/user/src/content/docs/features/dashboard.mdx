The dashboard gives every active account its own card, currency, current balance, and month-to-date money flow. Below the accounts, expense, income, and net worth charts show how your finances change over a selected period.

<ScreenshotPair desktop={desktop} mobile={mobile} alt="Renalo dashboard with account balance cards" />

## What the numbers mean

**Total balance** starts with the account's opening balance, adds posted income and incoming transfers, subtracts posted expenses and outgoing transfers, and includes manual balance adjustments. Renalo uses today's date in your browser's timezone, so activity after your local today is excluded.

**Inflow** includes this month's posted income and incoming transfer amount for that account. **Outflow** includes posted expenses and outgoing transfers. The current month also follows your browser's timezone. Transfers use the amount belonging to each side's account currency.

Archived accounts are absent from the dashboard account cards but keep their records.

## Explore income and expenses

The dashboard initially shows **Last 12 months**. Use the date selector below the account cards to choose a month, the last 12 months, the current year, all time, or a custom range. The dashboard remembers this selection in your browser. Relative selections such as **This month**, **Last 12 months**, and **This year** are saved as relative choices, so they move forward automatically when you return later; custom dates remain fixed.

Dashboard ranges stop at today in your browser's timezone. Future dates cannot be selected here, and planned transactions are excluded even for **This year** and **All time**. Expense, income, and transfer overviews still allow future ranges for planning.

The expense and income charts use the same default-currency projections as their overview pages and automatically group totals by day, week, or month. The dashed line is a linear trend that makes the overall direction easier to see. Transactions without an available default-currency conversion are omitted from these charts. Use the maximize button in a chart's top-right corner to open it across the full page.

### Follow net worth

The **Net Worth** chart shows the combined closing balance of every account in your current default account currency. Unlike the account cards, it retains archived accounts and their transactions because those records remain part of your financial history. Internal transfers do not increase or decrease net worth, while income, expenses, and balance adjustments do. Future activity is excluded.

For an opening balance in another currency, Renalo approximates its default-currency value from the available transaction conversion rate nearest that account's earliest activity. Foreign-currency adjustments use the available transaction rate nearest the adjustment date. If no transaction conversion is available for a foreign opening balance or adjustment, that value cannot be included. Each daily, weekly, or monthly point is the balance at the end of that period, including activity before the selected range.

### Save chart views

Use the gear beside a chart's maximize button to create a named view for that chart. A view can include only selected categories or accounts, or exclude selected categories or accounts. Exclusion is useful for removing unavoidable spending such as taxes while continuing to include new discretionary categories automatically. You can also choose automatic, daily, weekly, or monthly grouping.

Expense and income views are independent. Selecting a view changes only that chart; the balance cards and the other chart are unaffected. The dashboard date range also remains separate and applies to every chart view.

Renalo saves named views and each chart's active selection to your account, so they follow you across browsers and devices. Choose **All expenses** or **All income** from the gear menu to return to the built-in unfiltered view. The same menu lets you edit or delete the current named view.

The view editor keeps category and account rules independent. Choose **Include only** to chart selected values, or **Exclude** to remove selected values while automatically retaining new categories or accounts.
