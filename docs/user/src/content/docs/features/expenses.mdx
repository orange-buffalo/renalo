Each expense belongs to one account and one expense category. Amounts are positive values; the expense type determines that they reduce the account balance.

<ScreenshotPair desktop={desktop} mobile={mobile} alt="Expense overview with posted and planned entries" />

## Record an expense

1. Select **Add expense**.
2. Choose the date, category, and account.
3. Enter the amount in the selected account's currency.
4. Add optional notes.
5. Enable recurrence when this expense follows a schedule.

The category dropdown lists the categories with the most associated expenses first. Categories with the same number of expenses are ordered by name.

Dates are date-only values. Today's and past entries affect current balances; future entries appear as planned activity.

## Find the right entries

The global date range is shared across transaction and transfer overviews. You can use future months for planning, but selecting more than 12 months or **All time** shows a warning because loading a large amount of data might make the page unresponsive. **More filters** narrows expenses by account, category, or notes, while archived accounts and categories are excluded from selectors. On mobile, these filters open in a full-page panel; use the close button to return to the overview without clearing your selections.

<ScreenshotPair desktop={dateWarningDesktop} mobile={dateWarningMobile} alt="Large date range warning in an overview filter" />

Category and account filters start at **All**, with every current option checked and no selection tags. Clear a checkbox to exclude that option, or use **Only** on any row for a quick one-item filter. Clearing the final checkbox or removing the final selection tag resets the filter to **All**. Selecting every current option also returns to unrestricted **All**, so historical entries linked to archived options remain included.

The area chart uses the same date range and filters as the expense table. Renalo automatically uses daily, weekly, or monthly totals to keep longer periods readable, fills calendar gaps with zeroes, and shows all available values in your default account's currency. Foreign expenses use their saved comparison value based on actual transfers; expenses without conversion evidence are omitted from the chart. Hover a point to see its date and formatted total, or use the maximize button to open the chart across the full page. The chart is visible by default on desktop and hidden by default on mobile; use the chart icon beside the grouping control to show or hide it.

Use the grouping control beside **More filters** to switch between a plain list, groups by date, or groups by expense category. Date and category headers show the combined amount for each currency on the right, without mixing currencies into one total. Renalo remembers the selected expense grouping on this device independently from the income overview.

On mobile, tap a compact row card to reveal secondary details and edit/delete actions; the date remains visible while collapsed. Tapping the **Planned expenses** card immediately shows its planned entries instead.
