Each expense belongs to one account and one expense category. Amounts are positive values; the expense type determines that they reduce the account balance.

<ScreenshotPair desktop={desktop} mobile={mobile} alt="Expense overview with posted and planned entries" />

## Record an expense

1. Select **Add expense**.
2. Choose the date, category, and account.
3. Enter the amount in the selected account's currency.
4. Add optional notes.
5. Enable recurrence when this expense follows a schedule.

The category dropdown lists the categories with the most associated expenses first. Categories with the same number of expenses are ordered by name.

Dates are date-only values. Today's and past entries affect current balances; future entries appear as planned activity.

## Record a refund

On the Dashboard, open **Record new** and select **Refund** (the last menu item). Choose the refund's currency, enter the full amount, and select **Find matches**. Renalo searches all your accounts in that currency, including archived accounts, without currency conversion. Only exact amounts match; partial refunds are not supported.

<ScreenshotPair desktop={refundFormDesktop} mobile={refundFormMobile} alt="Refund form explaining how refunded expenses are removed" />

The search starts with **Last 6 months**. You can choose **Last 3 months**, **Last 6 months**, **Last year**, **Last 2 years**, or **Full history**. Ranges include their starting date and end today in your browser's timezone, independently of the overview's date filter.

<ScreenshotPair desktop={refundPeriodDesktop} mobile={refundPeriodMobile} alt="Refund search period choices" />

Matches can be a single expense, a single recurring payment, or a whole recurring series with at least two recorded payments. For a series, all payments through today must add up to the refund amount and use the selected currency. The series needs a payment in the selected date range, but older payments still count toward its total. Future payments do not count.

Each option shows the dates, amounts, accounts, categories, notes, and recurring schedule, plus how many expenses will be removed. Select the correct option and review the warning before selecting **Record refund**:

- **Single expense:** permanently deletes that expense.
- **Single recurring payment:** deletes that payment and every following occurrence, including future payments, and ends the schedule there. Earlier occurrences remain.
- **Whole recurring series:** deletes every occurrence, including older and future payments, and stops the schedule.

This records a refund by removing spending, not by creating income. Deletion cannot be undone. If expenses change after your search, Renalo asks you to search again before saving.

<ScreenshotPair desktop={refundSelectionDesktop} mobile={refundSelectionMobile} alt="Selected recurring refund with payment details and permanent deletion warning" />
<ScreenshotPair desktop={refundSeriesDesktop} mobile={refundSeriesMobile} alt="Whole recurring series refund showing all recorded payments and cancellation of future payments" />

If no expenses match, check the amount and currency or try a longer period. **Cancel** returns to Expenses without changing anything.

## Find the right entries

The global date range is shared across transaction and transfer overviews. Use the month arrows or a custom range to plan into the future. Selecting six months or more, including **This year** and **All time**, shows a warning because loading a large amount of data might make the page unresponsive. **More filters** narrows expenses by account, category, or notes, while archived accounts and categories are excluded from selectors. On mobile, these filters open in a full-page panel; use the close button to return to the overview without clearing your selections.

<ScreenshotPair desktop={dateWarningDesktop} mobile={dateWarningMobile} alt="Large date range warning in an overview filter" />

Category and account filters start at **All**, with every current option checked and no selection tags. Clear a checkbox to exclude that option, or use **Only** on any row for a quick one-item filter. Clearing the final checkbox or removing the final selection tag resets the filter to **All**. Selecting every current option also returns to unrestricted **All**, so historical entries linked to archived options remain included.

The area chart uses the same date range and filters as the expense table. Renalo automatically uses daily, weekly, or monthly totals to keep longer periods readable, fills calendar gaps with zeroes, and shows all available values in your default account's currency. Foreign expenses use their saved comparison value based on actual transfers; expenses without conversion evidence are omitted from the chart. Hover a point to see its date and formatted total, or use the maximize button to open the chart across the full page. The chart is visible by default on desktop and hidden by default on mobile; use the chart icon beside the grouping control to show or hide it.

Use the grouping control beside **More filters** to switch between a plain list, groups by date, or groups by expense category. Date and category headers show the combined amount for each currency on the right, without mixing currencies into one total. Renalo remembers the selected expense grouping on this device independently from the income overview.

<ScreenshotPair desktop={filtersDesktop} mobile={filtersMobile} alt="Expense filtering controls" />

On mobile, tap a compact row card to reveal secondary details and edit/delete actions; the date remains visible while collapsed. Tapping the **Planned expenses** card immediately shows its planned entries instead.
